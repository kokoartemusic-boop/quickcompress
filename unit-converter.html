How to convert units online

Using this converter takes only a few seconds. First choose the type of measurement from the first menu: length, weight or temperature. Then type the number you want to convert into the value box. Pick the unit you are starting from in the From menu and the unit you want in the To menu. The answer appears right away and changes as you type, so there is no convert button to press. If you chose the wrong direction, use the swap button between the two menus to flip them. You can convert kilometers to miles, kilograms to pounds, Celsius to Fahrenheit and many other common combinations.

Why use this unit converter

Different countries use different measurement systems, and it is easy to make a mistake when you convert by hand. Recipes, travel distances, body weight, shipping packages and weather forecasts often use units that you may not use every day. This tool does the arithmetic for you using standard conversion factors, and it works on phones, tablets and computers. The calculation runs in your browser on this page, and you do not need to create an account or install anything. It is useful for students, travelers, cooks and anyone who works with measurements.

Frequently asked questions

Is this unit converter free?

Yes. You can use it as often as you like without paying and without creating an account.

Which units can I convert?

You can convert length (kilometers, meters, centimeters, millimeters, miles, yards, feet and inches), weight (tonnes, kilograms, grams, milligrams, pounds and ounces) and temperature (Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin).

How accurate are the results?

The converter uses standard conversion factors and shows up to eight significant digits, which is accurate enough for everyday use. For scientific or legal work, please check the result against an official source.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Choose Temperature, enter the value, select Celsius in the From menu and Fahrenheit in the To menu. The formula used is multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32.
