How to calculate percentages online

This page has three calculators, one for each of the most common percentage questions. In the first, type a percentage and a number to find that percentage of the number, for example 20 percent of 150. In the second, type two numbers to find out what percent the first one is of the second, for example 30 out of 150. In the third, type an old value and a new value to see the percentage increase or decrease between them, which is useful for prices, salaries, grades and statistics. The answers appear right away and change as you type, so there is no button to press. Each box starts with an example that you can replace with your own numbers.

Why use this percentage calculator

Percentages come up in shopping discounts, tips, taxes, interest, school marks and business reports, and a small slip in the arithmetic can lead to a wrong decision. Frequently asked questions

Is this percentage calculator free?

Yes. You can use it as often as you like without paying and without creating an account.

How do I find a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply the result by the number. For example, 20 percent of 150 is 20 divided by 100, times 150, which equals 30.

How is percentage change calculated?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide the result by the old value, then multiply by 100. A positive answer is an increase and a negative answer is a decrease.

Why can I not use zero in some boxes?

Some calculations divide by one of your numbers, and dividing by zero has no answer. The second calculator needs a second number that is not zero, and the third needs an old value that is not zero.

The calculations run in your browser.
