How to generate a strong password

Creating a password with this tool takes a few seconds. A new password appears as soon as the page loads. Use the slider to choose the length, from 8 to 64 characters. Then tick or untick the boxes to decide which kinds of characters to include: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and symbols. Every time you change a setting, or click the Generate button, you get a fresh password. When you are happy with it, click Copy and paste it into the password field of the website or app you are signing up for. The strength line under the password shows a rough estimate based on its length and the characters used.

Why use a random password generator

People often choose passwords based on names, dates or words from the dictionary, and these are easier for attackers to guess. A random password has no pattern to guess. Longer passwords with a mix of character types are much harder to crack than short ones. This generator uses the secure random number generator built into your web browser, and the password is created on this page, so this tool does not send it to a server. For important accounts such as email and banking, use a long password that you do not reuse anywhere else, and consider keeping your passwords in a password manager so you do not have to remember them.

Frequently asked questions

Is this password generator free?

Yes. You can generate as many passwords as you like without paying and without creating an account.

Are my passwords stored or sent anywhere?

No. The password is created in your browser and this tool does not send it to a server or save it. When you close or refresh the page, it is gone, so copy it before you leave.

How long should my password be?

Many security guides suggest at least 12 to 16 characters. For important accounts, a longer password of 16 characters or more with different types of characters is a better choice.

What does avoiding look-alike characters do?

It removes characters that are easy to confuse, such as the capital letter I, the lowercase l, the number 1, the capital O and the number 0. This helps if you need to read or type the password by hand.
